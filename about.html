Niels Aage works on multiphysics design and optimisation at the Technical University of Denmark. His research asks what structures and devices should look like when their functionality determines the shape, from full-scale aircraft wings resolved with billions of voxels to hearing instruments, microwave filters and patient-specific implants. The aim is to use material where it matters most, giving structures and devices that perform better, use less energy and carry a smaller climate footprint.

The work spans fast parallel solvers and optimisers, multiphysics modelling, and open-source codes used in research, industry and teaching worldwide.
